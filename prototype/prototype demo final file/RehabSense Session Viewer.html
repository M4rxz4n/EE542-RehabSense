How to read this page

The sleeve measures two things while the wearer does slow biceps curls, and the firmware decides which movements count as reps.

Elbow angle
How far the elbow is bent, from two motion sensors on the upper arm and forearm. 0° is a straight arm; about 90° is the forearm at a right angle.
Biceps effort
Muscle activity picked up by the EMG electrodes, shown as a percentage of the hardest squeeze recorded during calibration. 100% is that maximum squeeze; under % the muscle counts as relaxed.
Rep
One curl. It starts when the elbow rises past °, counts only if it reaches °, and ends when the arm drops back below °.
Partial rep
A curl that started but stopped short of °. It is shown but not counted.
Lift, hold, lower
The three parts of a rep. A controlled curl takes about 2 s to lift, 1 s held near the top and 3 s to lower, around 6 s in all. These are general defaults; a therapist may set different ones.
Fault
A moment where a motion sensor failed to answer. Readings during a fault are unreliable.

Recording a session log

  1. Bluetooth (rehabsense_v5): open this viewer in Chrome or Edge on a laptop, press Connect sleeve and pick RehabSense. The charts fill in as you curl, and Download log saves everything received. Inside Claude's page viewer Bluetooth is blocked, so use the standalone viewer file there (the panel above offers it). On a Mac, allow Bluetooth for Chrome in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
  2. Serial: upload the firmware, open the Serial Monitor at 115200 and type l so each line carries the device time, velocity, rep state and upper-arm tilt.
  3. Quick way: after the session, click in the Serial Monitor output, press Cmd+A then Cmd+C, and use Paste log here.
  4. For long sessions, record straight to a file. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor, then in Terminal run:
    cd ~/Desktop && screen -L /dev/cu.usbserial-0001 115200
    Commands such as c, l and e work there too (typing doesn't echo). Quit with Ctrl-A, then K, then Y. Open screenlog.0 from your Desktop here.
  5. Keep the laptop unplugged from its charger while the electrodes are on.
